ACCOUNT & PRIVACY

Local sign-in.

Connect this PC to your Tapo bulbs.

Tapo account

Used to authenticate directly with bulbs over your local network.

⌑
Before you sign in

Add each bulb in the Tapo phone app and finish its setup first. Make sure the bulbs and this PC are on the same Wi-Fi network. Enable Third-Party Compatibility in the Tapo app if prompted.

After setup, use Find lights on the My lights page to discover them here.

Where it goes: your credentials are used from this PC for local bulb authentication, then stored encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Tapo Deck does not send them to a cloud service or telemetry endpoint. The bulb receives the local authentication exchange.

ABOUT

Tapo Deck.

A local-first Stream Deck plugin for Tapo smart lights.

✦

Built for your home setup

Discover compatible bulbs, authenticate locally, and control power, brightness, white temperature, and color from customizable Stream Deck keys. The bundled engine uses python-kasa and runs on this PC while the plugin is active.
